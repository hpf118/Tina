# Business plan: personalized science comics

Planning documents for turning the personalized science-comic prototypes into a business.

- **[`business-plan.md`](business-plan.md)** — full plan: honest assessment (including the third-party character IP problem and the fix: the child's avatar, real friends and family, and the child's own life experiences as the story), product ladder, market, marketing plan, unit economics and three-year scenarios, legal and privacy checklist, China strategy, risks, milestones.
- **[`action-plan-90-days.md`](action-plan-90-days.md)** — week-by-week plan for the first 90 days.
- **[`Personalized-Science-Comics-Business-Plan.docx`](Personalized-Science-Comics-Business-Plan.docx)** — both documents plus the prototypes as one Word file (cover, table of contents, appendix). Rebuild with `node build-docx.js` after editing the Markdown (needs the `docx` npm package; run `npm install docx` in this folder first).
- **[`prototypes/`](prototypes/)** — the five original prototype comics (Wonderland physics, water cycle, rainbows) kept for internal reference. They contain third-party characters (Peppa Pig, Peanuts, Canada's Wonderland) and must not be published or sold.
