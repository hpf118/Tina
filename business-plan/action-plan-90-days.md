# 90-Day Action Plan

Companion to `business-plan.md`. Assumes about 10 hours per week from the founder and 5 from a spouse. Every week has one outcome that can be checked as done or not done.

## Weeks 1 to 2: Clear the legal path

| Week | Actions | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Book the values and ethics meeting at your department. Draft a one-page description of the business (products, time commitment, that no government resources or content are involved). | Meeting booked; page written |
| 1 | Shortlist three brand names. Search each on CIPO, USPTO, CNIPA, domain registrars and YouTube. Check the intended Chinese-character version too. | One name chosen |
| 2 | Register a federal corporation. Both spouses as shareholders. Sign a short shareholders' agreement that assigns all IP each of you creates to the corporation. | Certificate of incorporation received |
| 2 | Engage a trademark agent. File the word mark and logo in Canada, then via the Madrid Protocol in the US and China (classes 16 printed matter, 9 downloadable content, 41 education and entertainment). | Filing receipts in hand |

## Weeks 3 to 5: Build the avatar system and mascot

| Week | Actions | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Write the character brief: the avatar system (child and companion avatars from toddler to grandparent: skin tones, hair, glasses, hijab, wheelchair, common pets) and one original mascot. Describe the mascot's personality and the tone (warm, funny, curious). Send to three illustrators with a copyright-assignment clause. | Three quotes received |
| 4 | Choose an illustrator. Approve rough sketches. | Roughs approved |
| 5 | Receive final character sheets (front, side, three expressions, two poses each). Train the consistent-character model on the sheets. Generate a test page and compare to the sheets. | A generated page that a stranger would say matches the sheets |

## Weeks 4 to 8: Rebuild the prototypes and prove the pipeline

| Week | Actions | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | Rewrite the Rainbow Mystery (Levels 1 and 2) script on the new model: the child and a companion of the family's choosing, the mascot as guide, and an opening panel built from the parent's two-sentence "what happened". Add the try-at-home experiment page and the parent page. | Two scripts approved by a second educator |
| 5 to 6 | Lay out both levels at print quality (bleed, 300 dpi, CMYK). Order proof copies from two print-on-demand suppliers. | Proofs in hand; supplier chosen |
| 7 | Rebuild the Wonderland set as "a day at the amusement park" with the family naming the park. Rebuild the Puddle Mystery as "a rainy walk". | Three titles at two levels ready |
| 8 | Set up ordering: Shopify (or equivalent) plus an order form that collects name, grade, avatar choices, the experience, the two-sentence "what happened", and up to three companions. Manual fulfilment is fine for the first 50 orders. Publish privacy policy and terms (lawyer-reviewed). | A test order flows from payment to a delivered PDF |

## Weeks 6 to 10: Build the audience

| Week | Actions | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | Launch the one-page waitlist site: the story, three spreads, the experience menu, a free sample comic in exchange for email, and a CAD 10 reservation for a founding-family book. | Site live |
| 6 | Record the first founder video: why an education scientist built a physics comic for her six-year-old. Post to YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn. | Posted |
| 7 | Interview ten parents of children aged 5 to 10, at least three you do not know. Ask: what do you want your child to get from a book like this, what would you pay, printed or digital, what would make you buy a second one. | Notes written; prices adjusted |
| 7 to 10 | Spouse storyboards and animates episode one (3 to 5 minutes) with the mascot and a sample family. | Episode one published |
| 8 | Post two short clips per week: a 45-second science explanation for parents, and a behind-the-scenes clip. Pin the printable worksheets on Pinterest. | Cadence held for three weeks |
| 9 | Reach out to the Ontario Science Centre, Ingenium, and two Ottawa-area libraries about a launch event or a co-branded edition. | Two conversations held |
| 10 | Post the free Level 1 rainbow comic on Teachers Pay Teachers with the school licence described. | Listed |

## Weeks 11 to 13: Founding families

| Week | Actions | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 11 | Close the waitlist at 50 reservations (or by date). Collect personalization details. | 50 orders |
| 12 | Generate, check and ship all 50 printed books. Include a hand-written note and a card asking for a photo and one sentence. | All shipped |
| 13 | Collect photos and quotes. Measure: how many replied, net promoter score, what they said the child did with it. Build the public website from these. Decide go or no-go for public launch in month 5. | Decision made using the gates in `business-plan.md`, Section 13 |

## Tracking sheet

Keep one spreadsheet with these columns, updated weekly:

- Waitlist emails, reservations, orders, revenue
- Cost of goods per order (print, shipping, generation)
- Marketing spend and cost per order
- Repeat purchases and referrals
- Hours spent by each founder
- Open legal items (employer disclosure, trademarks, contracts)

The numbers that decide the business at the end of 90 days are: reservations per 100 waitlist emails, the share of founding families who send a photo unprompted, and your actual hours per title.
